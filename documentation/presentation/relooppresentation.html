The challenge

What if waste wasn't the end of the story?

Every day, useful materials are thrown away — not because they have no value, but because the right people don't always find each other.

Today

A broken connection

  • People don't know where to take recyclable waste.
  • Collectors and recyclers are hard to discover.
  • Good recycling efforts often go unnoticed.
A better possibility

A connected recycling community

  • Make recyclable materials easier to find.
  • Bring people and collection services together.
  • Celebrate actions that help the environment.
Our idea

Meet the marketplace for a more circular world.

ReLoop brings the recycling journey into one welcoming place — from sharing materials to collection, exchange, and recognition.
